# Code of conduct

Everyone who participates in Altimatrix issues, pull requests, reviews or other project spaces should be able to contribute regardless of background, identity, disability or experience. Explain technical disagreements with evidence, give credit, and offer specific feedback someone can act on.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual attention, and publishing someone’s personal information without permission are unacceptable. This applies to maintainers as well as contributors, including when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and response

Contact a maintainer privately using a contact method they publish on their GitHub profile. If a private contact is unavailable, use GitHub’s **Report abuse** action on the relevant content or profile. Include links, dates and the outcome you need. Share personal information only with the people handling the report.

Maintainers should hear from affected participants, limit access to the report, and recuse themselves when personally involved. Responses may include requesting a correction, removing content, restricting participation or banning a participant. Retaliation for raising a concern is itself a violation.
